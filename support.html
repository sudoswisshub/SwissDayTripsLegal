Frequently asked

How do I restore my Pro subscription on a new device?

Open Swiss Day Trips → Settings, and tap "Restore Purchases." Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple ID you used to subscribe. Restoring doesn't create a new charge.

How do I cancel or change my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple, not inside the app. On your device, go to Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions, choose Swiss Day Trips Pro, and change or cancel your plan there. Access continues until the end of the period you've already paid for.

Why does the app ask for my location?

Only if you tap "Use my current location" when searching. It's used once, for that search, to find day trips starting near you — it isn't collected or stored. You can deny access in Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services without losing any other feature.

Do my saved trips and notes sync across devices?

Favourites, notes, and the Home Screen widget share data on the same device through an app group, but nothing is synced to a server we run. If you switch devices, restore your subscription and your saved data will follow only if it's included in your iCloud device backup.

A departure time or route looks wrong — what should I do?

Schedules, routes, and weather come from independent public providers (transport.opendata.ch, geo.admin.ch, open-meteo.com, OpenStreetMap). If something looks off, always double-check with the operator before you travel, and let us know at alice.turcanu91@gmail.com so we can look into it.
